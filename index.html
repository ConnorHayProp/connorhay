About

One drive,
every arena

I've spent my life competing — as a Protea Karate Athlete, and now in Mixed Martial Arts, and Triathlons. That same discipline is what I bring to real estate: I'm a Candidate Property Practitioner at McIntosh Properties based in Hillcrest. My goal is to help buyers, sellers, renters, landlords, and investors navigate the KZN market with the same preparation I bring to competition day.

I'm currently completing my BCom in Investment Management, as well as becoming a Certified Associate In Project Management — building toward a future where I can help clients not just buy and sell property, but build long-term wealth through smart investment strategies. Property is where I'm starting. Where I'm going is even bigger...
